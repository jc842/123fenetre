# Thermal Insulation & Energy Savings: How New Glazing Cuts Heating Bills

Windows account for up to 15% to 20% of an uninsulated home's total heat loss. Upgrading from single glazing to modern insulated glazing yields immediate energy dividends.

---

## 1. Key Metrics: Understanding Uw and Ug
- **Ug (Glass)**: Measures heat transmission through the glazing unit.
- **Uw (Whole Window)**: Measures total heat loss across both glass and framing. The lower the Uw value, the better the insulation. Target Uw ≤ 1.3 W/m²K for optimal performance.
- **Sw (Solar Factor)**: Ratio of solar energy entering the room. High Sw allows winter passive solar heating.

## 2. Low-Emissivity (Low-E) Coatings
Microscopic metal oxide layers applied to interior glass panes reflect interior radiant heat back into the living room while allowing visible daylight to enter freely.

## 3. Acoustic Insulation Upgrades
Asymmetrical glass configurations (such as 10/16/4 or laminated acoustic stadip silence) block street noise, aircraft traffic, and city rumble far more effectively than standard symmetrical panes.
