# Soundproofing Windows: Acoustic Glazing for City Living & Quiet Nights

Urban noise pollution can severely degrade sleep quality and daily concentration. Acoustic window replacement provides significant decibel attenuation.

---

## 1. How Sound Travels Through Windows
Acoustic weakness in older joinery typically stems from two culprits:
1. Thin single glass panes vibrating under low-frequency rumble.
2. Degraded frame perimeter joints and sash play allowing airborne noise infiltration.

## 2. Asymmetric vs Acoustic Laminated Glass
- **Asymmetric Glazing (e.g., 10-16-4)**: The differing mass between the two panes disrupts harmonic resonance, eliminating frequency peaks.
- **Acoustic PVB Laminate (Silence Glass)**: Interlayers of acoustic polyvinyl butyral absorb vibration shocks and attenuate up to 42 dB of street noise.

## 3. Professional Installation is Crucial
Even the most expensive acoustic window will fail if the frame-to-wall perimeter joint lacks high-density expanding polyurethane sealants and acoustic caulking.
