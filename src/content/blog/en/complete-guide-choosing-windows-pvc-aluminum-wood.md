# The Complete Guide to Choosing Windows: PVC, Aluminum or Wood?

Upgrading your home's windows is one of the most cost-effective investments for thermal comfort, soundproofing, and energy savings. Selecting the right frame material determines durability and thermal efficiency.

---

## 1. PVC Windows: The High-Efficiency Benchmark
- **Thermal Performance**: Outstanding naturally insulating properties with multi-chamber profiles (Uw down to 1.1 W/m²K).
- **Maintenance**: Virtually zero maintenance; resistant to UV rays, coastal salinity, and moisture.
- **Cost**: The most affordable solution offering the highest return on investment.

## 2. Aluminum Windows: Sleek Contemporary Aesthetics
- **Slim Sightlines**: Narrow profiles maximize natural daylight intake by up to 15%.
- **Structural Strength**: Ideal for large sliding doors, oversized picture windows, and bay windows.
- **Thermal Break Technology**: Modern polyamide thermal breaks eliminate heat bridging.

## 3. Wood Windows: Authentic Character & Natural Insulation
- **Heritage Architecture**: Mandatory in historical preservation areas and Haussmannian facades.
- **Natural Warmth**: Exceptional acoustic damping and timeless tactile quality.
- **Maintenance**: Requires micro-porous stain or paint renewal every 5 to 8 years.

## 4. Double vs. Triple Glazing
Standard 4/16/4 Low-E Argon double glazing satisfies most climate zones. Triple glazing is recommended for north-facing alpine walls and passive house standards.
